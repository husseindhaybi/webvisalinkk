# Design System: Visa Linkk Lebanon

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Beirut Travel Poster"**

The page is a silkscreened travel poster from 1960s Beirut, printed in a handful of flat inks on warm paper stock. Every surface is one ink laid flat; photographs are printed as smooth two-ink plates; frames are Lebanese arches; a fine print grain sits over everything. Depth comes from inks overlapping, never from light and shadow.

Colour tells the time of day. The page opens on a morning sky, passes through paper and four poster sheets, into a sand afternoon, a teal dusk that darkens to indigo as the visitor scrolls, and closes on a starred night with a crescent moon. Any new surface places itself somewhere on that day and takes that ink as its ground.

Gold comes from the client's logo and is rationed: it marks what the visitor can do next and how far they have come, plus the logo's own swoosh and plane. The two brand inks (indigo and gold) are the logo's, and they govern over any earlier proposed value.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Flat ink fields, one per section, sequenced as a day from morning to night.
- Smooth two-ink duotone photographs (no dot screen); the full-colour original appears only on hover.
- Arch-topped frames (full half-round tops, square feet) for photographs and scenery.
- Jost (Futura lineage) in Latin; Reem Kufi display and Readex Pro text in Arabic, fully RTL.
- Pill-shaped controls, square-cornered poster sheets.
- A fixed poster margin and one 12-column print grid for the whole page.
- Print grain overlay at 7.5% over every surface.

## Colors

A limited silkscreen set: two logo inks, four daylight grounds, one paper stock, and two derived text tints.

### Primary
- **Logo Indigo** (indigo): the main ink. Body text and headings on light grounds, the destination band, the first poster sheet, arch backings behind prints, the active language pill, the scrollbar thumb.
- **Deep Indigo** (indigo-deep): step-number discs in the stacked (mobile) flight rail.

### Secondary
- **Action Gold** (gold-bright): the action ink. Primary buttons, the floating WhatsApp button, the current step in How it works, the flown route and reached waypoints, the intro progress line, text selection, and focus rings on dark grounds.
- **Lit Gold** (gold-hover): hover state for gold actions only.
- **Logo Gold** (gold): the logo's darker gold. Used for the hero swoosh that the gold plane flies along, and the text caret. It is the brand mark's colour, not an action colour.

### Tertiary
- **Sea Teal** (teal): the dusk ground where How it works begins, the cedar ridge in the hero scenery, and the second poster sheet.
- **Morning Sky** (sky): the hero ground and the third poster sheet.
- **Afternoon Sand** (sand): the Why section ground, the fourth poster sheet, and the lettering on the indigo sheet.
- **Sun** (sun): flat discs only: the hero sun, the dusk sun sinking into the sea, and the night crescent.

### Neutral
- **Paper Stock** (paper): the page's default ground (Services), the nav once scrolled, the service sheet drawer, the paper peaks in the hero, the frame around the hero arch, and text on dark grounds.
- **Night** (night): the Next Step section, mobile menu and intro screen; dark text on gold.
- **Deep Night** (night-deep): the footer and the html backdrop.
- **Soft Indigo** (ink-2): secondary text on paper, sky and sand (body copy, intros, list items).
- **Pale Lilac** (paper-2): secondary text on night and teal (captions, footer labels, steps not yet reached).
- **Indigo Rule** (rule-dark) and **Paper Rule** (rule-light): hairline rules and inset strokes on light and dark grounds respectively.

The dusk sequence in How it works animates its flat grounds through fixed stops with scroll: sky `#1f6e7a` to `#1a4a6c` to `#150f40`, sea `#175a64` to `#143a58` to `#0e0a2e`. At any moment each is still one flat fill.

### Named Rules
**The Gold Is Law Rule.** Action Gold marks only an action, the visitor's progress (current step, flown route, reached waypoint), or the logo's own swoosh and plane. It is never a section ground, never body or heading text, never decoration.

**The Colour Is Time Rule.** Each section is one flat ground chosen from the day: sky (morning), paper, sand (afternoon), teal to indigo (dusk), night. A new section takes the ink of its place in the day; it does not invent a new ground.

**The Dim By Colour Rule.** Secondary or not-yet-reached text is dimmed by switching to Soft Indigo or Pale Lilac, never by opacity, so it keeps 4.5:1 on its ground.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Jost Variable (with Readex Pro Variable, system-ui)
**Body Font:** Jost Variable (with Readex Pro Variable, system-ui)
**Arabic Display Font:** Reem Kufi Variable (large display only)
**Arabic Body and Heading Font:** Readex Pro Variable

**Character:** Geometric Futura-lineage lettering with the clean confidence of mid-century travel posters, set medium-weight and tight at display size and heavy capitals where a destination is lettered onto a sheet. In Arabic, Reem Kufi carries the posters' display voice and Readex Pro keeps text and mid-level headings calm and legible.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (500, clamp 2.7rem to 5.9rem, 0.98, -0.028em): hero headline and the closing call to action. Balanced wrap.
- **Headline** (500, clamp 2.2rem to 4.1rem, 0.98, -0.028em): section titles, held to roughly 12 to 14ch.
- **Poster Lettering** (800, 13.5% of sheet width, 0.92, -0.01em, uppercase): the destination word across the foot of each service poster sheet. Arabic: 700, 11.5cqi, 1.25.
- **Title** (500, clamp 1.4rem to 1.9rem, 1.15): service titles and step titles; uses the heading family (Readex Pro 600 at 1.4 to 1.45 in Arabic).
- **Lede** (400, clamp 1.06rem to 1.22rem, 1.55; 1.85 in Arabic): section intros, max 46ch.
- **Body** (400, 1.0625rem, 1.6; Readex Pro 1.03rem at 1.85 in Arabic): running copy, 32 to 54ch.
- **Label** (600, 0.78rem, 0.18em tracking, uppercase; no tracking in Arabic): factual labels only, such as footer field names and the photo caption pill.

### Named Rules
**The Kufi For Size Rule.** In Arabic, Reem Kufi is used only at display and headline sizes; every mid-level heading switches to Readex Pro via the heading family.

**The No Tracking In Arabic Rule.** Arabic sets letter-spacing to 0 everywhere and opens line-height (display 1.22, body 1.85).

## Layout

One print grid for the whole page: a 12-column grid with a fluid gap (16 to 32px) inside a 1320px content width and a fixed poster margin (16 to 56px). Sections breathe with a generous fluid pad (96 to 176px). Compositions are asymmetric on that grid: hero copy over 7 columns with the arch in the last 5; service posters staggered in 7/5 and 5/7 spans with the right-hand sheet dropped, like sheets pinned to a wall; Why pairs a 5-column triple arch with 6 columns of copy.

At 940px and below every block spans all 12 columns, the posters stack (at 86% width, alternating sides, between 600 and 940px), the nav collapses to a burger, and How it works becomes a stacked vertical rail. At 560px and below hero and closing buttons go full width. The nav bar is 76px (64px at 720px and below). Logical properties are used throughout so RTL mirrors the whole layout; directional art (swoosh, route, planes, scenery) is flipped with `scaleX(-1)` in RTL.

## Elevation & Depth

The system is flat. There are no cast shadows. Depth comes from inks overlapping in print order: the sun disc behind the arch, paper peaks and a teal cedar ridge in front, an indigo wave line at the foot; a night disc laid over a sun disc to make the crescent. `box-shadow` appears only as an inset 1 to 1.5px stroke on outlined controls and as the 1px rule under the scrolled nav. Layering is also expressed by a single translucent night scrim (`rgba(14,10,46,0.55)`) behind the service drawer, and the fixed print grain over everything.

### Named Rules
**The Print Order Rule.** Things sit in front of each other the way inks are laid on a poster: flat shape over flat shape. Never a blur, glow or offset shadow.

## Shapes

Three forms carry the system. **Arches:** photographs and scenery windows are full half-round topped with square feet (`999px 999px 0 0`); the hero arch sits inside a 9px paper frame, the Why section splits one image across three arches over an indigo sill. **Pills:** every interactive control (buttons, language toggle, burger, close button, floating WhatsApp, the photo caption) is fully rounded. **Sheets:** poster sheets and prints are square-cornered, as trimmed paper. Circles are reserved for the sun, moon and step numbers. Focus rings are 2px solid, 4px offset, 4px radius, indigo on light grounds and Action Gold on dark.

## Components

### Buttons
Confident printed pills, 54px tall (42px small, 60px large), 600 weight, with an arrow that slides out and a twin that slides in on hover.
- **Shape:** full pill (999px).
- **Primary (gold):** Action Gold with Night text. Hover lightens to Lit Gold. Press scales to 0.97.
- **Line (ink / paper):** transparent with a 1.5px inset stroke in the current colour; hover floods the pill with that ink and inverts the text.
- **Floating WhatsApp:** 60px gold pill fixed bottom-inline-end; the label unfolds beside the icon on hover-capable devices, touch shows the icon only.

### Service Poster Sheets (signature)
Each service is a full poster sheet of one flat ink (indigo, teal, sky, sand in that order) with the two-ink print inset and the destination lettered heavy across the foot in Poster Lettering. Sand letters on indigo, paper on teal, indigo on sky and sand. Prints are 7:6 on the wide sheets, 4:5 on the narrow ones. On hover the full-colour original grows out of the pointer position in a circular reveal (520ms in, 1100ms out) while the print zooms to 1.045. Below the sheet: title, Soft Indigo body, and an underlined "Ask about this" link whose underline retracts on hover.

### Service Drawer
A paper sheet sliding in from the inline end (max 580px) over the night scrim, with a pill close button, groups separated by indigo hairlines, and list items marked by a short 9px indigo dash.

### Navigation
Transparent over the hero, turning to Paper Stock with a 1px Indigo Rule once scrolled, and sliding away on scroll-down. Links are 500 weight with a 1.5px underline that draws in from the reading start on hover. The mobile menu is a full Night panel with display-size links and a Paper Rule above its footer.

### Language Toggle
A pill track with an inset hairline holding EN and the Arabic option; the active option sits on a sliding indigo pill (paper pill on dark grounds). Switching languages blurs the page out (190ms, 6px blur), swaps, and settles.

### Destination Band
A 64px indigo strip of uppercase tracked destination names separated by small paper planes at 55%, drifting in the reading direction (46s loop, paused on hover). It sits at the foot of the hero, where the hero's sea meets it.

### Flight Path (signature)
The logo's gold plane on a drawn swoosh. In How it works it flies a dotted paper route between three waypoints as the section is scrolled (pinned on desktop, a vertical rail on phones and with reduced motion); the flown route, reached waypoints and the current step number turn Action Gold, completed steps keep a gold ring.

### Why List
Rows of 30px single-weight line icons (drawn on one 28px grid) with medium text, separated by indigo hairlines that draw in from the reading start.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** give every section one flat ground from the day sequence: sky, paper, sand, teal to indigo, night.
- **Do** print photographs as smooth two-ink duotones and frame them in arches or poster sheets; keep the colour original for the hover reveal only. A visible halftone dot screen was tried and rejected by the owner: at screen size it reads as perforation, not print.
- **Do** reserve Action Gold (#d2a04f) for actions, progress and the logo's plane and swoosh.
- **Do** dim secondary text by colour (Soft Indigo on light, Pale Lilac on dark), never by opacity.
- **Do** make every control a pill and every sheet square.
- **Do** build with logical properties and flip directional art in RTL; Arabic takes Reem Kufi for display, Readex Pro for everything else, with zero tracking.
- **Do** ease with the shared curves (ease-out `cubic-bezier(0.23, 1, 0.32, 1)` for most transitions) and stop ambient loops under `prefers-reduced-motion`.

### Don't:
- **Don't** paint a visible colour gradient on any surface; transitions between grounds happen over time (scroll), not across space.
- **Don't** use cast, drop or glow shadows; `box-shadow` is only for inset strokes and the nav's 1px rule.
- **Don't** use gold as a section ground, heading colour or ornament.
- **Don't** set Reem Kufi below headline size or track Arabic text.
- **Don't** place work off the 12-column grid or outside the poster margin.
- **Don't** add uppercase tracked labels above headings; the Label style is only for factual field names and captions.

# Surface brief: Landing page (src/App.jsx)

Scope: the single bilingual (EN / AR-RTL) landing page. Mode: Persuade.
Audience: Lebanese travellers and families, mostly on phones, anxious about visas; job is to understand the four services and message the office.
Action: WhatsApp chat first, consultation booking second. Proof: honest process + bilingual personal help; no invented stats or testimonials.
Constraints: disclaimer always visible in footer; contact details are placeholders in `src/config.js`; stock photos are placeholders.
Memorable moment: a plane taking off from Beirut on the logo's swoosh and flying the visitor's journey from morning to night.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Visa Linkk sold the way 1960s Beirut sold travel: a silkscreened poster you want to step into. Refuses the category default of a passport-and-map photo hero with a centred serif title and icon cards.

OWN-WORLD: Flat inks, no gradients, no cast shadows: indigo #1E1650, action gold #C08A3E (only actions and the current step), sea teal #1F6E7A, sky #BCD7E3, sand #E7CFA4, paper #F4F0E8. Photos printed as two-ink duotones. Lebanese arch windows as frames. Jost (Futura lineage) + Reem Kufi / Readex Pro for Arabic. Halftone grain. One print grid, fixed poster margin.

STORY: Morning in Beirut (hero) → four service posters → sand afternoon (why us) → teal dusk (how it works) → indigo night (next step). Visitor learns what we do, trusts the honesty, taps WhatsApp.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Sky field; left 6 cols headline at ~5.5rem, "How can we help you?", paragraph, gold "Explore Our Visa Services" + ink "Contact Us"; right, a tall arch holding a duotone Raouché photo over a sand sun, gold swoosh with a plane crossing it; destination band scrolling at the base.

FORM: Beirut golden-age travel poster, grounded candidate 4 of 7; seed key befe1252. Raises: rank by weight/case/rule (timetable); no cast shadows (chaekgeori); colour as time (night city); gold is law (plankton); nothing off-grid (arcade). Signature interaction: pinned "How it works" flight path, plane flies between three waypoints, active step turns gold.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

React + Vite, static single-page landing site (no backend). Node.js is used only for tooling/build. Contact happens through WhatsApp, phone, and email links. Deployable to any static host (Vercel / Netlify).

## Users

People in Lebanon (Arabic- and English-speaking) planning to travel or move abroad: tourists needing a visa for Europe or elsewhere, people joining a partner in Australia, and people exploring Australian employer-sponsored work and training pathways. Many have had a refusal and want to understand next steps. They arrive anxious about paperwork and cost and want a trustworthy, human guide.

## Product Purpose

Visa Linkk Lebanon is a visa and travel-support consultancy. The landing page explains what the office helps with, builds trust, and moves the visitor to start a conversation (WhatsApp first, consultation booking second). Success = a visitor understands the services within seconds and contacts the office.

## Positioning

A Lebanese office that gives personal, bilingual (Arabic/English) guidance through the visa process: clear explanation of steps and fees, practical document preparation and translation, and coordination with qualified professionals where required. Strong focus on Australian visas (visitor, partner, employer-sponsored work, training) plus worldwide tourist visas (e.g. Schengen/Europe).

## Operating Context

Visitors mostly arrive on phones (WhatsApp/Instagram/Google). The real workflow: enquiry → understanding options → preparing documents → coordinating the next stage. Documents include passports, translations, invitation letters, and supporting files.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Services: Australian Visa Services; Worldwide Tourist Visa Assistance; Refusals & Appeals (consultation); Document Preparation & Translation.
- Bilingual site with an English/Arabic toggle; Arabic renders fully RTL.
- Legal disclaimer must stay visible: "Visa decisions are made by the relevant government authorities. Assistance or consultation does not guarantee approval."
- Undecided / not yet provided: office address, phone number, WhatsApp number, business email, opening hours (placeholders until the owner supplies them).
- Must never promise or imply guaranteed approval.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: "Visa Linkk Lebanon" / "VisaLinkk" (double k is intentional).
- Logo: `Visa linkk lebanon Logo.pdf.pdf` — serif wordmark "VISA LINKK" in deep indigo, "LEBANON" in gold above it, with a gold airplane and swoosh.
- Brand colors from the logo: gold (~#A8742E) and deep indigo (~#2A1E6E).
- Owner's request: premium / luxurious feel with rich, beautiful animation and moving imagery.

## Evidence on Hand

- Logo PDF only. No real photos yet: stock travel imagery is used as placeholders and must be swapped for real office/team/client imagery later.
- No testimonials, client counts, approval rates, or partner logos exist. Never fabricate them.

## Product Principles

1. Honesty over hype: explain, never guarantee.
2. Human and personal: a person helps you, in your language.
3. Clarity first: every visitor should know the next step (message us) within seconds.
4. Calm confidence for an anxious visitor.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Full Arabic RTL support; respects `prefers-reduced-motion`; mobile-first.
